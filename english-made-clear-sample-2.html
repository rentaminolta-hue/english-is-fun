🔤 Chapter 1 — Key Sounds

Chương 1: Những âm thanh quan trọng

Many English sounds don't exist in Vietnamese. That's why pronunciation feels difficult! Here are the ones that trip Vietnamese speakers up most — and exactly how to fix them.

1. /θ/ — think, three, thank

👉 How: Put your tongue lightly between your teeth. Air flows through.

❌ Don't say this: "tink", "tree", "tank" — that's /t/, not /θ/

2. /ð/ — this, that, they

👉 How: Soft sound — tongue between teeth, but voice is ON (vibrating).

❌ Don't say this: "zis", "zat", "zey" — that's /z/, not /ð/

3. /r/ vs /l/ — red / led; rice / light

👉 /r/: Tongue pulled back, tip curled up → red, rice, right
👉 /l/: Tongue tip touches roof behind teeth → led, light, late

4. /v/ vs /w/ — very / well; wine / vine

👉 /v/: Top teeth touch bottom lip → very, vine, voice
👉 /w/: Lips rounded, no teeth touching → well, wine, welcome

⚠️ 5. Don't drop final consonants!

❌ Wrong✅ Correct
cacat
hehelp
gugood
friefriend

2.2 Introduce Yourself

✅ Correct:
"Hello, my name is Linh. I live in Hanoi. I work as a manager."

Xin chào, tôi tên là Linh. Tôi sống ở Hà Nội. Tôi làm quản lý.

❌ Don't say: "My name Linh. I live Hanoi. I work manager." — English needs connecting words!

2.3 Real Dialogue

A: Hello! Where are you from?
→ Xin chào! Bạn từ đâu đến?
B: I am from Vietnam. And you?
→ Tôi từ Việt Nam. Còn bạn?
A: I am from South Africa. Nice to meet you!
→ Tôi từ Nam Phi. Rất vui được gặp bạn!
